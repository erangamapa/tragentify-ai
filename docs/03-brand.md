## 03. Positioning

**The problem:** degen trading is an information problem before it is a trading problem. Forty groups, hundreds of accounts, launches every minute, most of it noise. Nobody can process that fast enough to act well — so they miss the one that mattered, or buy the loud one because it is the one they saw.

**The product:** your private team of agents, making the best trades on your rules — instead of wading through the noise across call groups and crypto Twitter. Lead with that. The is-and-isn’t lists below exist to stop the copy drifting, not to define the product against competitors.

**What it is not**

- A call group blasting the same ticker at five thousand people
- A signals service that deletes its losers
- A wallet tracker that copies strangers into rugs
- A sniper bot that buys anything with liquidity
- A chat window for asking an AI about its listed pairs
- A vault you deposit into and hope
- A black box that shows you a P&L number

**What it is**

- **A team that already has the whole picture** — the whole point
- A private team of expert agents, one channel per person
- Built for degen trading — fresh launches and memecoins
- Running the user’s mandate, not ours
- Willing to refuse the user their own bad idea
- Advisory or executing, switchable, revocable in one message
- **Yours** — a team, not a feed
- Covers the noise so you never have to wade through it
- Quiet by default — it speaks when it matters
- Publishing its losses alongside its wins

> **Two lines carry the positioning.** “Including when you’re the one asking” — the control balance in five words, and nothing in this category would dare write it. And “most days they’ll tell you there’s nothing worth buying” — a call group physically cannot say that, because its revenue depends on activity. If copy gets cut anywhere, keep these two.

## 04. Voice

Institutional and calm. It should read like a research note, not a pump channel — the restraint *is* the marketing, in a category that discredited itself with hype.

### Rules

- Numbers live inside sentences, never in giant stat tiles.
- Never promise, imply or illustrate returns. No sample P&L presented as typical.
- Say what it can’t do, plainly and early. That is the differentiator, not a weakness.
- Every statistic carries a source and a date.
- Don’t lead with “AI” or “agentic” on the homepage — the tagline is the one place that word earns its keep.
- If a line could appear in a call group’s marketing, delete it.

### Banned

Rocket emojis · “10x” · “moonshot” · “guaranteed” · “alpha leak” · countdown timers · fake scarcity · green-candle screenshots · anything implying affiliation with Robinhood, Telegram or any protocol named on the site.
